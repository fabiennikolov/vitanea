- Всички продукти са произведени по **оригиналната технология на проф. Христо Крачанов**

### Published work — and why aronia is in almost everything

His publication list runs from low-temperature Perkin and Claisen variants through pectin chemistry
to industrial food technology. Two entries matter directly to the storefront:

> Технологии за натурални плодови сокове от **арония, къпина и други горски плодове**,
> внедрени в редовно производство.

> Антоцианинови плодове и антиоксидантна активност. Синергизъм на антиоксидантната активност на
> някои плодове и билки и копигментация при антоцианиновите плодове — **влияние на сок от арония и
> екстракт от арония на стабилността на пигментите в сока на малина и ягода**.

**That second line answers a question the brief leaves open.** The client says aronia is in almost
every product *"заради полезните ѝ качества"*. Krachanov's own research gives a second, technical
reason: aronia juice and extract **stabilise the pigments in raspberry and strawberry juice**
through anthocyanin co-pigmentation.

So when a Vitanea strawberry nectar contains aronia, that is not padding — it is the founder's
published science applied to his own product. It is a far better story than a health claim, it is
completely legal to tell, and it turns "аронията е плодът, който влагаме в почти всички наши
продукти" from an assertion into a mechanism. **This belongs on the Науката page and in the
composition panel's explanatory copy.**

Other implemented technologies worth citing: acid de-esterification of apple pectin (Pernik and
Gotha), turbulent pectin extraction (both plants), foamed frozen fruit creams, microbial
polysaccharides including xanthan, and chromatographic purification of alkaloids.

### Why he matters to the brand

Known nationally as **„Бащата на аронията"** — the Father of Aronia. BNT frames him as
*"човекът, който превърна аронията в земеделска култура у нас"* — the man who turned aronia into
an agricultural crop in Bulgaria.

His specific contribution was **a juice-extraction technology that preserves aronia's bioactive
compounds.** That is the "функционалността идва от плода" pillar restated as engineering — the
function was not added, it was *preserved*. This is the intellectual core of the whole brief.

A detail worth using: after roughly forty years working with aronia he says he has personally eaten
*"не повече от 200–300 грама"* of the fruit, because it is so astringent. Which is exactly why he
engineered drinkable formats.

### The citable evidence base

**Denev, Kratchanova et al. (2018),** *Journal of Chemistry* 2018:9574587 — Laboratory of
Biologically Active Substances, IOCCP-BAS Plovdiv. Peer-reviewed analysis of **23 Bulgarian-grown
aronia samples** (2016–2017), **co-authored by the founder's wife.**

| Measure | Range |
|---|---|
| Total polyphenols, fresh fruit | 1,022–1,795 mg/100 g FW |
| Anthocyanins, fresh fruit | 284–686 mg/100 g FW |
| ORAC, fresh fruit | 109–191 µmol TE/g FW |
| **Sorbitol** | 6.5–13 g/100 g FW — **61–68% of total carbohydrate** |

Sorbitol being aronia's dominant natural sugar is a genuinely defensible, non-claim differentiator
for the diabetic-friendly positioning.

> ⚠️ The paper analysed **experimentally prepared** juices, **not Vitanea commercial products.**
> Do not imply otherwise.

## 7. 🚨 Health claims — the rules

**There are no authorised health claims for Aronia melanocarpa, anthocyanins, or fruit polyphenols**
under Reg. (EC) 1924/2006. Of ~2,758 claims submitted, 222 were authorised; of 230 antioxidant
claims submitted in 2008, **8** got a positive opinion. The only authorised polyphenol claim is for
**olive oil** polyphenols — not transferable. **"Antioxidant" is not itself a permitted nutrition
claim.** Disease claims are absolutely prohibited under Art. 7(3) of Reg. 1169/2011.

### ❌ Must not appear — all currently live on Vitanea pages

- „понижават кръвното налягане"
- therapeutic effects „при атеросклероза, лъчеви увреждания, ракови заболявания и интоксикация с тежки метали"
- „арониевият сок се препоръчва при базедова болест"
- „силен антиоксидантен и **лечебен** ефект"
- „извежда радиоактивни вещества и тежки метали от организма"
- unqualified „естествен източник на антиоксиданти" / „природни антиоксиданти"
- the invented **„3,000–5,000 ORAC units per day"** daily-requirement framing
- „подходящ за диабетици" — regulated dietetic wording, needs legal review
- „запазва 80% от полезните вещества" — unverifiable quantified benefit

### Two compounding evidence problems

1. **The USDA withdrew its ORAC database in 2012**, explicitly stating ORAC values have no proven
   relevance to human health — yet ORAC figures lead most Vitanea product descriptions.
2. **Blood-pressure claims are not supported.** The 2019 meta-analysis of RCTs (PubMed 31237052)
   found no significant effect on systolic blood pressure; the 2025 critical systematic review
   (*Nutrients* 17(9):1488) reports mixed and inconclusive cardiometabolic results.

Blood lipids fare somewhat better (reduced total and LDL cholesterol in meta-analysis), but that is
still not an authorised claim.

### ✅ Safe, and stronger for the brief

1. **Piggy-back authorised vitamin C claims** where composition is lab-verified per SKU
   (≥7.5% NRV per 100 ml for beverages): *„Витамин C допринася за нормалната функция на имунната
   система"*, *„…за защита на клетките от оксидативен стрес"*, *„…за намаляване на умората"*.
2. **Provable nutrition claims** — без добавена захар, с ниско съдържание на захари, източник на фибри.
3. **Neutral composition facts** — anthocyanin mg/100 ml, 100% fruit not from concentrate,
   342 aronia berries per bottle, sorbitol as aronia's main natural sugar, cold-pressed.
4. **Provenance and process** — Krachanov, the patents, УХТ Пловдив, BAS, the factory, zero-waste,
   the family. Fully legal, and far more differentiating than any health claim.

Science lives as **clearly separated editorial on the Науката page, citing peer-reviewed
literature** — never as product-page claims. Art. 12 restricts implied health references even
there.

> **This needs a Bulgarian food-law review before launch.** It is the single item most likely to
> force late copy rewrites across every product page.
